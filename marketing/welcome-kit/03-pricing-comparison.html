FBA Prep Market Pricing Comparison

Client reference for how we estimate competitive pricing — and why many national prep centers charge more once minimums and add-ons are included.

Research snapshot: October 2026 (industry index dated June 2026) · New Jersey, USA · www.prepservicesfba.com

How we talk about “market” pricing: PrepCorex client Reports use an internal typical-3PL FBA prep benchmark of $1.35 / unit (labeling + standard prep). That figure sits near the published US prep-center median and is used to estimate savings vs common market cards — not as Amazon’s fee, and not as a promise of every competitor’s invoice.

1. US market snapshot (published rate cards)

Independent directories that compiled dozens of verified US prep-center rate cards in 2026 report roughly:

  • Median entry rate: about $1.30 / unit
  • Middle 50% of published cards: about $0.99 – $1.66 / unit
  • A-la-carte FNSKU only: often $0.20 – $0.85 / unit when sold separately
  • Polybag: often $0.15 – $0.75 / unit when not bundled
  • Bundling / kitting: commonly $0.50 – $2.00+ / bundle on top of base prep

3. Why competitors often cost more in practice

  • Hard monthly minimums: e.g. MyFBAPrep Standard (~5,000 units/mo) means you pay for capacity even in slow months.
  • Headline “from $0.40” vs real basket: lowest published rates usually assume standard-size SKUs and omit bundling, fragile wrap, returns, and long-term storage.
  • Add-on stacking: polybag, bubble wrap, kitting, carton forwarding, and returns are frequently billed outside the headline per-unit rate.
  • Opaque quotes: many national 3PLs (including ShipBob-class networks) hide FBA prep behind custom contracts with software / setup / account fees elsewhere in the stack.

5. What to say when someone asks “how did you estimate pricing?”

  1. We track published US prep-center rate cards (median ~$1.30; common band ~$0.99–$1.66).
  2. PrepCorex uses $1.35 / unit as the typical 3PL FBA prep benchmark for savings estimates.
  3. We then quote Prep Services FBA for that merchant’s SKU mix, volume, and services (custom — not a one-size public card).
  4. We highlight competitor minimums and add-ons so the “cheap from” rate isn’t compared apples-to-oranges.
