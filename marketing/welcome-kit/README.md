## How to export PDFs for clients

1. Open each of `01`, `02`, and `03` in Chrome.
2. Print → Destination: **Save as PDF**.
3. Margins: Default; enable **Background graphics** for brand bars.
4. Share the three PDFs (optional short email cover). Do not send `index.html` unless the team needs the cover sheet.

## Research basis (how this kit was structured)

Industry best practice for B2B / 3PL welcome packets:

1. Warm welcome + who you’ll work with  
2. Agreed outcomes / value  
3. Onboarding timeline with owners  
4. What the client must provide (SKU, rates, ASN)  
5. Working / billing policies (short)  
6. One clear next action  

Plus a **service portfolio** and a **signed / discussed rate context** (here: market comparison + custom quote process).

Sources used while drafting:

- Valuecase / Portico / OnboardHive welcome-packet frameworks  
- 3PL onboarding checklists (rate card before first receive)  
- Live public copy from [prepservicesfba.com](https://www.prepservicesfba.com) and PrepCorex pages  
- Existing internal pricing HTML under `marketing/pricing-comparison/`
