2. What happens next (first 48 hours)

  1. Kickoff & strategy review
    We confirm channels (Amazon FBA / FBM, Walmart WFS, Shopify, TikTok Shop, eBay), SKU profile, prep rules, storage needs, and volume targets.
  2. Account + PrepCorex access
    Your client login is created. You get inventory, inbound, outbound, and reporting visibility — $0 setup / $0 monthly portal fee.
  3. Rate card & billing setup
    Custom pricing is confirmed for your SKU mix and services. Billing contact and invoice delivery method are locked before first receiving.
  4. First inbound ready
    You send inventory (or schedule LTL / container). We receive, inspect, log, and route into prep / storage / fulfillment workflows.

3. What we need from you

Please send these before (or with) your first shipment so receiving is clean and fast:

  • SKU list — SKU / FNSKU / UPC, title, units per case, dimensions/weight if known
  • Prep instructions — polybag, bubble, bundling, expiration / lot / opaque bag rules
  • Channel mix — FBA vs FBM / DTC vs WFS; any special labeling
  • Inbound plan — first ASN date, carrier, carton/pallet/container count
  • Billing contact — AP email, company legal name, any PO requirements
  • Integrations — Shopify / Amazon / other store connections if used
Incomplete SKU or prep rules are the #1 cause of inbound delay at every 3PL. Sending a clear SKU sheet before freight arrives is the fastest way to hit your SLA.

4. How you’ll work day to day

In PrepCorex

Create inbound requests, track receiving, manage inventory, submit outbound / FBA shipments, watch live receiving when available, buy labels, and download reports.

With our warehouse team

We execute receiving, inspection, FNSKU / carton labeling, bundling, pick & pack, pallet prep, carrier handoff, and returns under structured ops workflows.

Communication

Email / phone / WhatsApp for urgent ops. Portal status is the system of record for inventory and shipment state — check PrepCorex before re-asking for updates.

Turnaround

Typical receiving / prep / ship windows are 24–72 hours depending on volume and complexity. Same-day dispatch is available for approved workflows and cutoffs.
