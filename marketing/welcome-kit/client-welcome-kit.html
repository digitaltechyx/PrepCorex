Pricing — kept simple

Every seller’s products are different, so we give you a custom quote. To keep pricing fair and clear, here is how the US market usually looks.

Quick market number: Most US prep centers charge around $1.00 – $1.65 per unit for standard FBA prep. The middle of the market is about $1.30 – $1.35 per unit. Your Prep Services FBA quote is made for your products — not a generic “from $X” ad price.
