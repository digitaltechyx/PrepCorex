Your privacy matters to us

This Privacy Policy explains how Prep Services FBA (“PSF,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website, services, or communicate with our team. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as information collected automatically when you use our website or services.

Information you provide

  • Name, business name, and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Amazon Seller Central information, store name, and selling account details
  • Shipping addresses, inventory details, and product information
  • Billing information and payment details (processed securely through our payment provider)
  • Communications you send via email, WhatsApp, or our contact forms
  • Any other information you choose to provide when requesting a quote or signing up for services

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Pages visited on our website, time spent, and referring URLs
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6)

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, manage, and fulfill our prep and fulfillment services
  • To process shipments, track inventory, and generate reports via PrepCorex
  • To communicate with you about orders, shipments, and account status
  • To send quotes, invoices, and service-related updates
  • To respond to inquiries, questions, and support requests
  • To improve our website, services, and internal processes
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights
  • To send marketing communications (only with your consent; you may opt out at any time)

3 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third parties (shipping carriers, payment processors, software platforms) who assist us in delivering services. They access information only to perform services on our behalf and must keep it confidential.
  • Amazon and marketplace platforms: We may submit shipment information to Amazon Seller Central or other platforms on your behalf, using credentials you provide.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of PSF, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers: In a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may transfer as part of that transaction. We will notify you before it becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • Secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our website
  • Restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular review of data collection, storage, and processing practices
  • Secure, reputable payment processors — we do not store full credit card details

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorized use of your account.

5 Data Retention

We retain personal information as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes in this policy.

  • Account and transaction data: for the duration of your relationship with PSF and up to 7 years afterward for legal and tax compliance
  • Communication records: up to 3 years after your last interaction
  • You may request deletion at any time, subject to our legal obligations to retain certain records

6 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance browsing, analyze traffic, and understand how visitors use our site.

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand site usage (e.g., Google Analytics). Data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Marketing cookies: Used for relevant ads and campaign performance, only with your consent.

You can control cookies in your browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality.

7 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion, subject to our legal obligations
  • Opt out of marketing: Unsubscribe from marketing emails or contact us directly
  • Data portability: Request your data in a portable format where technically feasible

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

8 Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites (e.g., Amazon Seller Central, carrier portals). We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content. Review their policies before providing personal information.

9 Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for business use by adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will promptly delete it.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Significant changes will be posted on our website with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
